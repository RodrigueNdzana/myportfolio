A developer who also thinks like a designer.

I'm a multidisciplinary technology professional working across web design, full-stack development, and software engineering. My path started in 2019 with a foundational course at the College of Cape Town, and continued through a diploma program at Cape Peninsula University of Technology - where I've built a solid base in Java, C#, Scala, and modern web technologies.
